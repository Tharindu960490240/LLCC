      Our Heritage

      A new club with a long game in mind

      The Launceston Lankan Lions Cricket Club was founded in 2026 to give Sri Lankan families and friends in northern Tasmania a home for the sport they grew up with. Cricket is how many of us first met each other here, and the club is how we plan to keep doing it.

      The lion on our crest nods to the Sri Lankan flag, and the red ball is the one we all chase. We welcome players of every age and level, from first-time bowlers to seasoned club cricketers.

      • Community first. Families, juniors and supporters are part of the team.
      • Play fair, play hard. Respect for umpires, opponents and the ground.
      • Open to all. You don't need to be Sri Lankan to roar with the Lions.
